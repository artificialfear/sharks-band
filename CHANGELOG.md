- ISLANDS BAND title is back, centered above the clock. The IHS BAND logo moved to the top-left corner.

## v1.0 (2026-11-11)
- First release: welcome screen with clock, a next-event countdown and a daily note.
- Tap a class to see its goal, plan and reminders. Tap plan items to cross them off.

- Settings for class names, the event and the note, plus backup export/import.
